Accounting & Tax ServicesSherwood Park & Edmonton, Alberta

Tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll and GST/HST services for individuals, self-employed professionals and corporations in Sherwood Park, Edmonton and across Alberta — prepared in line with the Income Tax Act and CRA requirements.

What We Offer

Trusted Expertise Across Every Filing

From personal T1 returns to full corporate year-ends, our team ensures your compliance obligations are met accurately and on time.

Personal & Corporate Tax

Accurate preparation and filing of T1 personal returns and T2 corporate returns, with full optimization of available deductions, credits, and carryforward provisions under the ITA.

T1 Returns T2 Returns RRSP & TFSA Capital Gains

Corporate Accounting

Full-cycle corporate accounting for CCPCs and other Canadian entities, including year-end close, CRA audit support, and integration with your existing accounting software.

CCPC Year-End Close CRA Audit Support

Financial Statement Preparation

Year-end financial statements compiled from your records using ASPE (Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises) as a framework, for tax filing, lenders and shareholders. Audit and review engagements are reserved to CPA firms licensed for public accounting in Alberta — if your lender requires one, we will coordinate with a licensed firm.

ASPE Year-End Statements Lender Packages

Business Payroll & GST/HST

Accurate payroll processing including CPP, EI, and income tax remittances, alongside GST/HST registration, preparation, and CRA remittance filings.

CPP & EI GST/HST Returns T4 Slips PD7A

Bookkeeping

Monthly and quarterly bookkeeping services that maintain accurate general ledgers, bank reconciliations, and accounts payable/receivable — keeping your books audit-ready year-round.

General Ledger Bank Rec QuickBooks

Tax Planning

Proactive tax minimization strategies including income splitting, holdco structures, small business deductions, lifetime capital gains exemptions, and estate freeze planning.

Income Splitting SBD LCGE Holdco Structures

CRA Correspondence

Help responding to CRA reviews, audit queries and reassessments, preparing notices of objection, and Voluntary Disclosures Program (VDP) applications. With your written authorization through the CRA's Represent a Client service, we can deal with the CRA on your behalf.

Objections Reassessments VDP Audit Response
