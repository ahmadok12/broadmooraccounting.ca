# Website review — November 2026 (SEO, structure, visual)

Follow-up to `AUDIT-2026-10.md`. Section 1 lists what was changed in this round; section 2 lists what is still worth doing, in priority order.

## 1. Changed in this round

### Navigation & design language
| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Mega menu** replaces the Services dropdown | 6 services with icons + one-line descriptions, a "Free consultation" feature card (phone + hours), and a calculators link. Opens on hover (mouse), click/Enter (keyboard/touch), ↓ moves into the panel, Esc closes and returns focus, closes on outside click / focus-out. Static HTML, so every link stays crawlable. |
| **Floating glass navbar** | Rounded pill, frosted glass (`backdrop-filter`), compacts on scroll; the top contact bar collapses on scroll and is hidden on phones (phone + WhatsApp move into the pill and drawer). Breakpoint for the hamburger raised from 768px to 1020px so the menu never overflows on tablets. |
| **Mobile drawer** | Floating glass panel with rounded corners, services accordion (real `<button>`s with `aria-expanded`), focus trap, Esc to close. Inline `onclick` handlers removed from the header. |
| **Sitewide glass language** (`assets/css/glass.css`) | Shared tokens (radii, glass surfaces, shadows). Heroes run under the floating header with rounded bottoms; dark sections ("Why…", comparison, calculators intro, map, info strip) become rounded islands; cards, FAQs, forms, calculators and tables become frosted glass; all buttons are pills; footer is a floating rounded panel. Loaded last on every page via a new `BUILD:theme` marker, with a content-hash `?v=` for cache busting. |
| Emoji icons → SVG | Nav, footer, homepage proof bar, calculator headers, contact hours/parking icons. Emojis render differently per OS and looked inconsistent next to the line icons. |
| Logo | Nav uses a 7 KB mark (was a 49 KB full logo shrunk to 58px, where the wordmark was unreadable) plus a text wordmark; footer mark 7 KB (was 25 KB). |

### SEO / performance
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Shortened 8 `<title>`s to ≤ 58 characters (were up to 81) | Google truncates around 580 px (~60 chars); the location keywords were being cut off. |
| Homepage H1 no longer starts at `opacity: 0` | The H1 is a Largest Contentful Paint candidate; a delayed fade-in pushed LCP back ~0.8 s. |
| Contact image on the homepage got `width`/`height`, `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"` | Prevents layout shift (CLS) and saves bandwidth below the fold. |
| Footer column titles `<h4>` → `<p>` | They skipped heading levels on every page and added "Services / Company / Get in touch" to every page's outline. |
| `fetchpriority="high"` removed from the nav logo | Only the hero image should compete for priority. |

## 2. Recommended next steps

### High impact
1. **Google Business Profile + reviews** (carried over) — the single biggest local-ranking factor for "accountant Sherwood Park / Edmonton". Then add `aggregateRating` only from genuine reviews.
2. **About / Team page** with real names, credentials (CPA, AIC licence, RECA), photos and licence numbers. This is a YMYL (finance) site; E-E-A-T is weak without people behind it. Link it from the nav ("About") and footer.
3. **Search Console** — verify, submit `sitemap.xml`, and check Core Web Vitals and indexing of all 12 URLs.
4. ~~**One page per core service intent**~~ Done for personal tax, corporate tax, bookkeeping/payroll and Super Visa (round 2). Next candidates: GST registration, CRA audit help, life insurance, first-time home buyer mortgages — — "personal tax return Sherwood Park", "corporate tax (T2) Edmonton", "bookkeeping Edmonton", "Super Visa insurance Edmonton" currently share one page each. Split the highest-volume ones into dedicated pages; the mega menu has room for a second column of sub-links.

### Structure / maintainability
5. ~~**Inline CSS duplication**~~ Done (round 2). Remaining: `pages/index.css` (60 KB) could be trimmed of unused rules — — every page carries 750–2,900 lines of its own `<style>` (index.html is 139 KB, ~100 KB of it CSS), much of it repeated nav/footer/form/FAQ rules. Move shared component styles into `style.css`/`glass.css` and keep only page-specific rules inline. Smaller HTML, browser-cached CSS, one place to edit.
6. **Google Fonts** are render-blocking. Self-host DM Sans + Playfair Display (WOFF2, `font-display: swap`) or at least drop unused weights (900, 300).
7. Add `geo` (lat/long) and `priceRange` to the `AccountingService` schema, and a `Person` entry for each team member once the About page exists.

### Content / GEO (AI search)
8. **Local guides** (1–2 per month): "Alberta AT1 for small corporations", "GST registration in Alberta", "Super Visa insurance requirements 2026". Short, factual, question-led headings — this is what AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) quote.
9. Add a short "Key facts" summary near the top of each service page (who it's for, what's included, how to start, fees approach). Answer engines extract these.
10. Consider an `llms.txt` at the root listing the service pages with one-line summaries.

### Visual
11. **Real photography** — the hero and advisor images read as stock/AI. Real team and office photos would make the glass design feel premium rather than generic.
12. Service-page heroes have an empty right half; a small glass card (e.g., "What to bring", "Typical turnaround", or a phone CTA) would balance them.
13. Re-add the partner/insurer logos and reviews from `tools/drafts/` once the client confirms them (see October audit) — they're strong trust signals and would suit glass cards.
