Key Dates

Tax Deadlines for Edmonton Individuals & Businesses

General federal deadlines that apply in Alberta. If a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, the CRA generally treats a filing or payment as on time if it is made the next business day. Your own deadlines depend on your situation and year-end.

WhenWhat is due
Last day of FebruaryT4 and T5 slips and summaries due to the CRA and to employees.
First 60 days of the yearRRSP contributions deductible against the previous tax year.
April 30Personal (T1) filing deadline for most individuals, and the payment deadline for everyone — including the self-employed.
June 15T1 filing deadline for self-employed individuals and their spouses or common-law partners (any balance owing was still due April 30).
15th of each monthPayroll source deductions due for most regular remitters.
2–3 months after year-endCorporate tax balance due (3 months for many Canadian-controlled private corporations claiming the small business deduction).
6 months after year-endCorporate T2 and Alberta AT1 returns due.
