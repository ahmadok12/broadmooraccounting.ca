Super Visa InsuranceFor families in Edmonton, Sherwood Park & across Alberta

Bringing parents or grandparents to Canada on a Super Visa? The application needs private medical insurance that meets Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) rules. We help you compare policies through advisors licensed with the Alberta Insurance Council, so the coverage is right for your family and accepted with the application.

At a Glance

Super Visa Insurance in Brief

Minimum coverage
At least $100,000 of emergency coverage for health care, hospitalization and repatriation.
Validity
Valid for at least 1 year from the date of entry, and for each entry to Canada.
Insurer
A Canadian insurance company, or an OSFI-authorized foreign insurer issuing the policy through its Canadian business.
Payment
Paid in full, or in instalments with a deposit. A quote alone is not accepted.

IRCC Requirements

What the Insurance Must Include

These are IRCC’s requirements for the medical insurance part of a Super Visa application. The application has other requirements too, including the host’s minimum income and an immigration medical exam.

RequirementWhat IRCC asks for
InsurerA Canadian insurance company, or a foreign insurer authorized by OSFI to offer accident and sickness insurance that issued the policy while doing insurance business in Canada.
CoverageHealth care, hospitalization and repatriation, with at least $100,000 of emergency coverage.
ValidityAt least 1 year from the date of entry, and valid for each entry to Canada. Renew it if it will expire before your parent leaves.
PaymentPaid in full, or by instalments that include a deposit. Quotes are not accepted.
DocumentsThe policy must name the insurance company that issued it, and border officers may ask to see it.
Length of staySuper Visa holders can stay up to 5 years per entry and can apply to extend while in Canada.

Source: IRCC, Super visa for parents and grandparents. Requirements can change — always confirm against IRCC’s current guidance before applying. Broadmoor does not give immigration advice; for help with the visa application itself, speak with a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer.
